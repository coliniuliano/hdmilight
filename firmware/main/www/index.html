Board

The TV counts as off once its HDMI hot-plug has been down for half a second. "TV on" and "TV off" send CEC Image View On and Standby; CEC must be enabled on the TV. The SPI route switches the capture chip off while it runs.

Reads or rewrites the MS2130's boot flash through its USB port (works with a blank flash too). Reading changes nothing; writing erases the flash first, and an image that fails its checksums is refused.
